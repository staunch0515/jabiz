A quiz that brings customers through your door

Publish a short quiz about your shop, your products or your town. People answer it on their phone; the winners collect a prize at your counter. No app to install, nothing to pay during the launch phase.

Open the merchant console Try a quiz as a taker

How it works

  1. Sign up as a merchant and describe your business.
  2. Write a quiz in minutes, or let the assistant draft it from your material; set the prize and how many can win.
  3. Share the link or print the QR code: on the counter, in the window, on a flyer, or mail it to your own customer list.
  4. People answer on their phone. A winner shows the result at your counter; you scan or show a code and the prize is collected.

What you get

Prizes, not payouts

In the launch phase rewards are prizes you hand over yourself: a coffee, a discount, a sample. Nothing moves through the platform.

Your own audience

Open the quiz to everybody near you, or to a list of e-mail addresses only: an invitation with a one-click link for each.

See what happened

Attempts, pass rates, prizes collected, by day and by quiz. Export the figures whenever you need them.

Three languages

The app and the console work in English, Chinese and Japanese; takers see your quiz in the language you wrote it.

Free during the launch phase

Merchants who join now use QuizBuks without charge while we build the service with them. Paid rewards and further services follow later and are announced to you first.

Questions? Sign up in the merchant console and use its help page, or write to the address shown there.
