How to use the app as a Taker. Paths are pages of the app (they start with /taker).

## Getting started

- Sign up with your e-mail address and open the link we send you; you can also sign in with a password or with Google. Verify your address before answering quizzes.
- Complete your profile (/taker/welcome): your name and your country or region. Quizzes are offered by country and region.
- The bottom of the app has Home (/taker/), History (/taker/history) and Account (/taker/account).

## Finding a quiz (Home, /taker/)

- Home lists the quizzes you can answer. Use Search, Sort (newest, reward, most completed…) and Filters (Sponsor, the Sponsor's country or region, reward, how many completed it).
- Quizzes you have answered are hidden; turn on "Show quizzes I answered" to see them.
- Shorts: a shelf of short videos on Home. Tap one to watch in full screen and answer from there.
- Topics you like: in Account → About me (/taker/account/details) choose topics, and turn on "Show only the topics I like" to limit Home and the shorts to them.
- Languages of the quizzes: Account → App settings (/taker/account/preferences). Unless you choose, they follow the app's language.
- Blocking: the "…" menu of a quiz offers "Not interested" and blocking every round of the quiz, the Sponsor or the video provider. What you blocked never shows and cannot be answered. Unblock it in Account → App settings.
- Some quizzes are only for people whose profile matches what the Sponsor looks for; telling more in About me may let you answer them. Sponsors never see your answers to About me.
- A quiz on a knowledge map shows once you have lit the nodes it needs. A quiz whose video cannot be played where you are now (for example YouTube in mainland China) says it is not available in your region.

## Answering (/taker/p/…)

- A quiz's page shows the number of questions, the time limit, the highest reward and how long it is open. "Start the quiz" starts the timer. Each person has one try.
- Some quizzes ask you to watch a video first. At the first launch there are no entry fees; once they open, some quizzes have an entry fee, paid from your Kudos that can be transferred, and not refunded whatever the result.
- Your answers are saved as you go. Submit when you are done; when the time is up, your saved answers are submitted.
- A survey has no right answers: answer every question to get its reward.
- The result shows your score, the pass mark, whether you passed, your reward and each question.
- The help cannot help with the questions or answers of a quiz.

## Rewards

Each quiz says how its reward is paid. At the first launch, Kudos are shown but cannot be used yet: Sponsors' rewards are cash, prizes collected at their place and digital prizes, not Kudos.

- Kudos (once they open): paid into My Kudos as soon as you pass. When the Sponsor's budget has run out, the reward is pending and is paid once they top up.
- Cash from the Sponsor: after passing you get a code. Show it when the Sponsor pays you, then confirm in the app that you received it, or report a problem. Until you confirm or report it in time, you cannot start new quizzes.
- Prize at the Sponsor's place: at their place, open the menu at the top right, tap "Scan" (/taker/scan), scan the code on their screen or poster and tap the prize you are collecting. Collect it before its deadline.
- Digital prize: a code, voucher or link appears in the app. Tap "Show prize", then confirm it works or report a problem.
- History (/taker/history) lists every quiz you answered with its result and reward.

## My Kudos (/taker/wallet)

- At the first launch, Kudos are shown but cannot be used yet: the Kudos at the top of the Me page show 0 marked "Coming soon", and nothing can be transferred or withdrawn. What follows is how it works once Kudos open.
- Your Kudos, what waits for Sponsors, what is held and what is on its way out. New rewards are held for a few days before they can be transferred.
- (Once Kudos open) Getting paid: Stripe (your Kudos are transferred to your Stripe account each day), or a bank account in Japan (/taker/wallet/bank: you ask for each withdrawal, a person checks it, and a fee is taken from each withdrawal). After you change the bank account, you can withdraw to it only after a waiting period.

## Messages and people

- Inbox (/taker/inbox): messages from the platform and announcements from Sponsors.
- Conversations with Sponsors (/taker/conversations): from the result of a quiz you completed, "Contact the Sponsor" asks about the quiz or your prize. Messages are plain text; do not share contact details. You can close a conversation, block the Sponsor or report the conversation.
- "Report this quiz" on a quiz's page tells us what is wrong with it; the Sponsor is not told who reported it.
- Refer a friend (/taker/referrals): some quizzes offer a referral link once you have answered or passed them yourself. If your friend passes in time, you get the referral reward.

## Account (/taker/account)

- Profile: your name and where you live. Your e-mail address cannot be changed here; contact us if it needs to change.
- About me: optional details for quizzes aimed at people like you, and the topics you like.
- My certifications (/taker/account/certificates): the certificates you hold and your public certification page (off unless you turn it on).
- Sign-in and security: password, two-step verification, linked accounts, signed-in devices and recent sign-ins.
- Notifications: which mail and push notifications you get, and the mail's language.
- App settings: languages, sound effects, what you blocked, installing the app.
- My ID and staff (/taker/account/id): your ID, which a Sponsor needs to add you to the staff who hand out their prizes. Staff work from the staff desk (/taker/staff).
- Become a Sponsor: in the account menu, once you have lit the knowledge map node that unlocks it. My Sponsor account (/taker/my-sponsor) shows a Sponsor's figures of today; everything else is done in the Sponsor app on a computer.
- Delete account (/taker/account/delete): the account is deleted after a waiting period; until then you can sign in and take the request back.

## Knowledge maps

- A Sponsor's knowledge map links quizzes. Passing a quiz lights its node; lit nodes can unlock other quizzes, a certification or a feature (such as referring friends or applying to become a Sponsor).
- My knowledge maps (account menu, /taker/maps) lists the platform's maps and each Sponsor's map you have answered or lit something in, with how many nodes you lit. Open one to see each node: lit, locked (light the nodes it needs first), can answer (with links to the quizzes), waiting for the next quiz, or conditions missing. Arrow keys move between nodes; the list view shows the same.
- A Sponsor's page (/taker/s/…) shows the Sponsor's map: signed in, with your own progress; signed out, only the nodes and their conditions.

## Help and terms

- Help centre with frequently asked questions: /taker/help. When the answer is not there, hand your question to a person.
- Ask the AI: at the top of the help centre (/taker/help, from Account → Help). It answers how to use the app, with links to the pages; mark an answer solved or not solved. "Ask a person" hands the conversation to the support team, whose reply comes there and in the inbox. Your earlier conversations are under "Your conversations".
