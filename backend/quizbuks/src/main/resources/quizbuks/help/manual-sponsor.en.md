How to use the Sponsor app as a Sponsor. Paths are pages of the Sponsor app (they start with /sponsor). Most work is done on a computer; the menu groups the pages into Overview, Content, Rewards, Sponsored benefits, Money and Settings.

## Applying and your Sponsor profile (/sponsor/profile)

- Every Sponsor is reviewed before publishing. Open the Sponsor profile, fill in the basics (company, introduction, logo) and apply; we mail you when it is decided. A sent-back application says why: change it and apply again.
- A Taker who already uses the app signs up here with the same e-mail address; the account becomes a Sponsor's too.
- The profile's sections: Overview (status and what is left to set up), Basics, Shop page (your page in the app), Staff (who hands out prizes) and Mail server (invitations sent in your name). Products are in the asset library (/sponsor/assets?tab=products).
- Profile package: export everything on these pages as one file, or import such a file. An AI tool can write one from your website with the prompt the page gives.
- If an operator looks after your account, they are your first contact.

## Company verification (/sponsor/profile/verification)

In "Sponsor hub → Company verification", enter your company's registered name and domain to show it is who it says by its own mail domain. If your account's address is on the domain (or a subdomain) it goes to your operator's review at once; otherwise enter an address on the domain and open the one-time link sent there (signed in to this account, within 48 hours; sending it again makes the earlier link stop working). Once your operator confirms it, you are verified and your public page shows "Verified company" with the domain. Public mail services' domains (Gmail, Outlook, QQ and the like) cannot be used, and one domain verifies one Sponsor only.
Once verified, the onboarding granted credit you received is topped up to the verified amount (once). Submitting a different name or domain ends the current verification at once; your operator or the platform may withdraw it, telling you why, and the credit given is kept.

## Dashboard (/sponsor/dashboard)

- Your application's status and how your publications are doing.

## Quizzes (/sponsor/quizzes)

- A quiz is a template: edit its draft as often as you like (materials such as articles, images, videos or PDFs, and the questions), then save a version when it is complete. Publications use saved versions.
- A quiz can be scored (pass mark) or a survey (no right answers).
- Questions can be imported from a file. When there are many, the page cuts them into batches by their number and by the size one request takes, so you do not split the file yourself. Large question banks can draw a set of questions for each Taker when you publish.
- Translations: offer a version in several languages.
- AI: AI can write candidate questions from the materials and translate texts; it uses your AI credit. You check every candidate before using it.
- Topics: give quizzes and publications topics from the platform's list, so Takers who like them find them.

## Publications (/sponsor/publications)

- A publication puts a saved quiz version in front of Takers. "New publication" opens the wizard: Quiz (the knowledge map node it lights, if any; name and version) → Time and rewards (period, marks, reward tiers) → Customer matching (who it is for) → Check and submit.
- Customer matching aims a quiz at Takers whose profile matches. Some conditions are charged per day; the wizard shows the estimated fee. Counts of matching Takers are rounded, so nobody can be singled out.
- Who may answer: everyone in the chosen regions, or only the e-mail addresses you invite (an invitation list; the invitations are sent from your own mail server, set up in the Sponsor profile).
- Rewards are paid in cash at your place, as a prize collected at your place, or as a digital prize (codes, vouchers or links). At the first launch, Kudos are not in use yet: a publication cannot offer Kudos as its reward. Once Kudos open, rewards can also be paid in Kudos from your wallet.
- Referrals and languages are set in the publication too. At the first launch there are no entry fees; once they open, they are set in the publication as well.
- Submitted publications are reviewed before they go on. While in review you can withdraw one to change it; once approved it cannot be changed (copy it to publish it again with changes). A sent-back publication says why.
- The page of a submitted publication has seven tabs, each with its own address: Overview, Quiz & version, Audience, Rewards, Fees, Statistics, and Share & QR code; the status and next steps (pause, copy, preview and more) are beside them. A draft still opens in the wizard.
- You can pause a publication and resume it (also from the app's My Sponsor account). Once Kudos open, Takers cannot start a publication that pays in Kudos while your wallet cannot pay its highest reward: top up.

## Knowledge map (/sponsor/publications/map)

- Link your publications into a knowledge map: passing one lights its node, and lit nodes open later quizzes or a certification. Publish the map so Takers see it.
- The page shows your map in effect and how many Takers lit each node (shown only from the statistics' minimum group up) and, counted once a day, how many answered its publications and have not lit it (with the day counted, under the same minimum), the publications bound and not ended, and whether a newer version is a draft, in review or was sent back (with the reviewer's reason). Takers see the map on your page.
- "Create my map" starts one; "Edit" opens the editor. Each node has a name, what lighting it gives, and its condition: all, any, or at least n of its items, each another node lit (of your map, or of a platform map Takers light, listed under the map's name) or a pass of a publication bound to this node. "Before answering" can be set only on node items of the outermost "all" (their quizzes stay hidden until those nodes are lit), and a pass at most once. Save the draft: the editor lists every problem by node. Then submit it: another member of your operator reviews it; a version sent back shows the reason, and a draft in review can be taken back. A node with publications still running cannot be removed. "Versions" lists every version; any of them can be shown.

## Challenges (/sponsor/profile/challenges)

- The "Challenges" tab of the Sponsor hub shows your operator's copies of the platform's maps: each node's state for you (lit, prerequisites not met, a challenge to answer, waiting for the next one), what lighting it opens (such as digital prizes or prizes collected in person) and its granted credit, and the challenges you can start. Choose a node to see its details and the link to answer.
- Answering is as for Takers: a countdown, saved as you go, submitted on the last question or when the time runs out. A challenge has no reward, and each is answered once.
- The result says your score and whether you passed. Once you pass, the node is lit (shown shortly after): the reward methods it opens can be chosen for your publications, and the granted credit goes to your platform fee account (once per node; it expires). If you did not pass, wait for your operator's next challenge.
- You can answer challenges once your Sponsor application is approved.

## Asset library (/sponsor/assets)

- Your images, videos, files and products in one place, with storage usage and the free amount.
- Shared videos: share a video with other Sponsors, or use one another Sponsor shares; its ledger of uses is under video uses.

## Rewards

- Cash rewards (/sponsor/cash-rewards): what you owe Takers in cash. Hand over the cash, mark it paid; the Taker confirms it in the app. Reports are decided by the platform.
- Digital prizes (/sponsor/digital-prizes): codes the platform hands out from what you loaded (delivered at once), and what you send yourself.
- Prize redeeming (/sponsor/redeem): show the code on this screen; a Taker scans it to collect the prize at once. A printed poster code works too, but you confirm each claim. Your staff can do this on their own phones; a Taker may also read out the six digits of their pass.

## Sponsored benefits (/sponsor/benefits)

- Buy other Sponsors' benefits for your Takers from the benefit catalog, offer your own benefits, talk in inquiries and follow your purchases.

## Money

- Platform fee account (/sponsor/money/account): your Granted credit and the cash you top up (by card, or by bank transfer where it is offered), which pay platform fees only. Invoices are in "Money → Invoices" (/sponsor/money/bills); unpaid ones are paid from here.
- Kudos: at the first launch, Kudos are not in use yet: you cannot turn cash into Kudos or Kudos back into cash, publications do not pay rewards in Kudos, and the menu has no Wallet. Once Kudos open, the Kudos in your wallet (/sponsor/money/wallet) pay the publications with Kudos rewards, and you turn cash into Kudos when needed.
- Moved here by your operator (an operator's direct Sponsor only): your operator's lead may move money from the operator's account into your platform fee account, shown as "From the operator". It pays platform fees only (after any Granted credit); it cannot be turned into Kudos or withdrawn.
- Prepayments: when you submit a publication, the fees it will be charged (today the audience rules) are set aside from your platform fee account as a prepayment (granted credit first, then cash). If the account is short, the submission does not go through; the "Funds" part of the wizard's last step lists each amount and the shortfall, with a "Top up" link. A prepayment is not a fee: it all comes back when the publication returns to its draft (withdrawn, rejected or its review expired), and what is left comes back within a day after it ends; what goes back to granted credit that has expired is voided at once. The publication's "Prepayment" section and the "Held in prepayments" line of the platform fee account show what is set aside.
- Distribution fee: Takers get every reward you set; the platform charges the distribution fee separately for each person who starts your quiz (whether they finish or pass). The price per start is fixed when you submit, from your operator's price and the publication's highest tier, and does not change after; a deposit is set aside then (the cap on winners times the price, with tax; without a cap, your operator's least deposit). On the 2nd of each month the starts of the month before are settled, from the deposit first and then your platform fee account; after the publication ends a last settlement gives the rest back. The publication's "Distribution fee" section shows the price per start, the people who started since the last settlement and about how much that is, and each month's settlement.
- Transactions (/sponsor/money/transactions): every movement, receipts and invoices; exportable.

## Settings and messages

- Page statistics (/sponsor/stats): how your shop page is visited.
- Messages (/sponsor/messages): conversations with Takers about your quizzes and prizes, and notices you send to Takers.
- Account (/sponsor/account): sign-in, two-step verification, notifications, language, and deleting the account.
