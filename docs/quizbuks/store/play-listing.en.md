## App name

QuizBuks

## Short description

Answer quizzes from shops and brands and win treats at their counter. Free.

## Full description

QuizBuks is the app where you answer quizzes from shops and brands and win treats at their counter.

■ Answer quizzes
・New quizzes arrive on your home page: coffee know-how, local topics, products and more.
・Answer one question at a time and submit. Stop any time and pick up where you left off.
・See your score and the right answers as soon as you submit.

■ Win prizes
・Reach the pass mark to win the prize the shop offers, such as a drink or a discount.
・At the shop, just scan the code on their screen or poster with your camera.
・See your prizes and the quizzes you answered any time in History.

■ Safe to use
・Every quiz is reviewed before it is published.
・Your answers are never shown to the shop that made the quiz; it sees only your display name, score and prize status.
・No ads.

■ Good to know
・For people aged 18 and over. Sign up with an email address or an account such as Google.
・Available in Japanese, Chinese and English.
・Prizes and quizzes are provided by each shop or brand. Google is not a sponsor of them and is not involved in any way.
