## App name

QuizBuks

## Short description

Answer quizzes from shops and brands to earn Kudos. Free, with results at once.

## Full description

QuizBuks is the app where you answer quizzes from shops and brands and earn Kudos.

■ Answer quizzes
・New quizzes arrive on your home page: coffee know-how, local topics, products and more.
・Answer one question at a time and submit. Stop any time and pick up where you left off.
・See your score and the right answers as soon as you submit.

■ Earn Kudos
・Reach the pass mark to earn the Kudos each quiz offers.
・Some quizzes also give in-store perks or digital prizes.
・Receive the Kudos you collect from the app's Wallet, for example by transfer to your bank account.

■ Safe to use
・Every quiz is reviewed by our team before it is published.
・Your answers are never shown to the shop that made the quiz; it sees only your display name, score and reward.
・No ads.

■ Good to know
・For people aged 18 and over. Sign up with an email address or an account such as Google.
・Available in Japanese, Chinese and English.
・Rewards and quizzes are provided by each shop or brand. Google is not a sponsor of them and is not involved in any way.
