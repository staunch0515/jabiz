# Official rules of reward campaigns

These rules apply to every quiz with a reward or prize on QuizBuks (the "Service"), each a "campaign". The conditions of each campaign (its reward tiers, the most winners, its dates, the regions it is for and how its rewards are received) are shown on its page. Where these rules and a campaign's page differ, its page prevails on that point.

## 1. Who runs a campaign

1. Each campaign is run by the Sponsor shown on its page, who decides its rewards and prizes and is responsible for providing them.
2. 【Operator name】 ("we"), who operates the Service, provides the platform on which campaigns run. Rewards paid in Kudos are given by us out of funds the Sponsor has paid in beforehand.

## 2. Apple and Google

**Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors of any campaign and are not involved in any campaign in any way.** The App Store and Google Play only distribute the Service's app; questions about a campaign go to us or to its Sponsor.

## 3. Who may take part

1. Takers who have registered on the Service and verified their email address may take part. A campaign may be limited to some regions.
2. Minors take part with the consent of a parent or guardian.
3. Take part with one account per person.

## 4. How to take part, and cost

1. You take part by answering the quiz. No purchase or payment is necessary.
2. The cost of connecting to the internet is yours.

## 5. How rewards are decided

1. A reward is decided by the tiers shown on the campaign's page (a reward for a range of scores) when you submit your answers within the time limit.
2. Once the most winners or the Sponsor's budget is reached, or the campaign has ended, later answers receive no reward. Whether a limit was reached is decided in the order in which our servers received the answers.
3. You may answer each campaign once.

## 6. Receiving a reward

A campaign's reward is received in one of these ways:

- **Kudos**: credited to your wallet in the Service; withdrawals follow the terms of use.
- **Cash paid by the Sponsor**: the Sponsor pays you against the code shown. Confirm it in the Service when you receive it, or dispute it if you do not.
- **Collected at the Sponsor's place**: collect it there before the date shown.
- **Digital prize**: delivered in the Service.

A reward or prize not received by its deadline may no longer be received.

## 7. Disqualification

If answers were obtained by cheating, through several accounts, with automated tools, by sharing answers or by any other unfair means, we or the Sponsor may disqualify the taker and cancel their rewards and prizes.

## 8. Changing or ending a campaign

The Sponsor or we may change, pause or end a campaign because of cheating, reports, natural disasters or other unavoidable circumstances. Rewards and prizes already decided are handled as section 6 says.

## 9. Personal information

Takers' personal information is handled under the privacy policy. A Sponsor is given only what handing over a reward or prize needs (the display name and the state of the reward).

## 10. Taxes

Any tax on a reward or prize is the taker's to declare and pay.

## 11. Contact

Questions about a campaign: quizbuks@gmail.com. If something is wrong with a quiz, use "Report this quiz" on its page.
