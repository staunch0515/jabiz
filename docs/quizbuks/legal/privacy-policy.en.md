### 2.2 Information from sign-in with another service

When you sign in or sign up with Google, Facebook or LINE, we receive from that company your identifier at that service, your email address (and whether it is verified) and your display name. We do not receive your password or anything else.

### 2.3 Information collected automatically

- Sign-in records: time, outcome, the site you signed in to, IP address and browser type (user agent)
- The IP address and browser type of each signed-in device (session)
- Records of operations in the Service: when, by whom and which operation. Secret values such as passwords are not recorded.

We do not collect your location (GPS or similar) or estimate it from your IP address. We use no advertising and no third-party behavioural analytics.
When you scan a code in a shop with the app's "Scan", the camera's picture is used on your device only to read the code, and is neither stored nor sent (the service only opens its own page the code links to).

To introduce the Service to businesses, we and our Operators (see 5) collect what a business publishes on its website: its company or shop name, its products, its website's language and country, and the business contact email address it publishes. We do not collect anyone's private email address, nor anything about a business whose website says it refuses advertising or promotional email.

## 3. How we use the information

2. To show Takers the quizzes available in their country or region
3. To show a quiz, and let a Taker answer it, only if what the Taker entered in About me meets the conditions the Sponsor set for it
4. To verify identity, and to prevent and investigate unauthorised sign-ins, improperly obtained rewards and other misuse
5. To meet legal obligations, such as issuing receipts and keeping accounting records
6. To send notices about the Service, including changes to the terms and this policy, and to answer enquiries
7. To produce statistics that do not identify individuals, and to improve the Service
8. For our Operators to review, manage and support Sponsors (see 5)
9. To introduce the Service to businesses (using the information under 2.4: an email with a trial quiz made from the business's own products; every email says how to stop them, and a request to stop holds for good)

## 4. What Sponsors see

When a Taker answers a Sponsor's quiz, that Sponsor can see:

- **Attempts:** the Taker's display name, score, and the reward and fee. Sponsors do not see the Taker's email address, ID or individual answers; each Sponsor sees a different pseudonymous identifier for the same Taker.
- **Rewards and prizes:** the Taker's display name and the status of cash rewards and digital prizes.
- **People the Sponsor invited:** the email address the Sponsor entered, and that person's progress, score and reward.

**Staff:** to register a Taker as staff, a Sponsor can look up that Taker's display name with the ID the Taker gives them. The Sponsor can then see the prizes that staff member hands over.

**About me:** what Takers enter in About me is not given to Sponsors. When setting conditions for a quiz, a Sponsor sees only how many Takers meet them: "fewer than 20" below 20, otherwise the number rounded down to the nearest 10.

Per-question analysis, such as the share of correct answers, is shown only as totals, and only once a minimum number of people (5 by default) have answered.

**What Takers and the public see**

- Takers see each Sponsor's company name, contact name and logo next to its quizzes.
- A quiz's share-link page can be viewed without signing in. It shows the quiz title, reward, number of questions, time limit and the Sponsor's company name.

## 7. Information stored on your device

The Service does not use cookies. It stores the following in your browser's storage (localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB):

- whether you are signed in
- your display settings (light or dark appearance, language, list layout)
- the temporary state of a quiz you are answering

The Takers' app also stores its page shell in the browser so that pages open offline. The shell contains no user information. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of these.

## 8. How long we keep information

- Account information, attempt and reward records, and sign-in and operation records are kept after an account is closed, as a record of transactions and to prevent misuse.
- Receipts, accounting records and notices from payment providers are kept for 10 years from the end of the fiscal year.
- AI-suggested drafts are deleted 7 days after they are generated.
- Uploaded files are fully deleted when the Sponsor deletes them.
- What you entered in About me is deleted as soon as you change or clear it. After an account is closed, it is no longer used to match quizzes.
- Businesses' information under 2.4 and the record of our introductions are deleted 1 year after the last introduction. When a business asks us to stop, we keep only its email address and domain, without a time limit, so that we never contact it again.

## 9. Security

To prevent leaks, loss or damage of personal information, we use:

- encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- hashing of passwords and encryption of secrets
- two-step verification for administrators, and access limited by permission
- records of operations, with tamper detection
- approval by more than one person for important changes to settings
